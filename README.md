# Google Merchant Center Missing Shipping Costs: Troubleshooting Guide

Google Merchant Center helps e-commerce businesses display their products across Google Shopping. However, missing shipping costs and incorrect delivery information can cause product warnings, disapprovals, and reduced shopping visibility.

These issues often occur when shipping settings, product feed attributes, and website checkout information do not match.

This repository explains common causes and practical checks that can help businesses troubleshoot missing shipping cost errors.

## Why Are Shipping Costs Missing in Google Merchant Center?

Google needs accurate delivery information to determine how much customers will pay to receive products.

Shipping-related errors may occur because of:

- Missing shipping services for targeted countries.
- Incorrect shipping rates or delivery regions.
- Incomplete product feed attributes.
- Incorrect product weight or dimensions.
- Differences between Merchant Center settings and website checkout.
- Outdated shipping information submitted by feed management applications.

For example, a store may offer free shipping above a specific order value, but Merchant Center may still contain an outdated fixed shipping charge.

## 5 Ways to Troubleshoot Missing Shipping Costs

### 1. Review Shipping Settings

Check your Merchant Center shipping and delivery configuration. Verify the targeted countries, delivery regions, shipping services, handling times, and applicable rates.

### 2. Compare Website and Merchant Center Information

Check whether the shipping charges displayed on product pages and during checkout match your submitted delivery information.

### 3. Verify Product Feed Attributes

If shipping calculations depend on product weight or dimensions, make sure those attributes are complete and accurate.

### 4. Check Feed Synchronization

Third-party applications and automated feed management systems can sometimes submit outdated information. Review their synchronization settings and update schedules.

### 5. Monitor Product Diagnostics

Regularly inspect Merchant Center diagnostics to identify affected products and new shipping-related warnings.

## Why Product Feed Accuracy Matters

Incorrect shipping information can affect product eligibility and create unnecessary campaign interruptions.

E-commerce businesses should maintain consistent product information across their websites, checkout systems, and shopping feeds.
